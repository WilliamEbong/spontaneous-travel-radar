# Surface brief: the dashboard (index.html) and the scoring table (table.html)

Scope: the whole dashboard, its health sheet and settings panel, and the scoring table page. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience: William on a phone each morning, on a laptop for detail. Job: read today's verdict, scan the best deals,
open one trip's evidence. Content: the collector's JSON and the routine's notes, unchanged. Constraints: static
files, no build step, vanilla JS, phone width first, both colour schemes.

Chosen direction: William's archive-of-looking system, pinned by him on 2026-11-11. Memorable moment: the night limb
of the Earth sitting on the page's one horizon under the verdict.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboard is a plate from an archive of looking: a radar is an instrument, and what it watches is the
planet's cities at night. It refuses the category default of white cards with a blue accent, coloured pills and a
status chip in a sticky app bar.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory paper (#F3EFE4) with fine grain, cream slips (#E9E1CD) with 1 px ink keylines at 30%, ink text,
graphite and caption grey for secondary text, rust (#A2573B) as the only payoff colour, ochre (#B98A3E) for the
no-day-off class rule, Prussian blue for the price line. Perpetua (Cormorant Garamond) for every headline, city
name and score numeral, never bold; Franklin Gothic (Libre Franklin) for text and labels; Consolas (IBM Plex Mono)
for every figure, date, caption and source. Square corners everywhere; no shadows, pills, badges, eyebrows or
uppercase labels. Dark scheme: charcoal ground, ivory text, faded ochre accent.

STORY: Within a second the visitor reads the verdict in serif above the Earth's limb; scrolling down the paper they
find the few trips that passed as catalogue drawers with a big score numeral each, then every city as a tray, then
the day's note as a page specimen. Any score opens its evidence as a field-note slip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-viewport night frame. Mono byline top left (Travel Radar · Calgary), mono scan time top
right, the health pulse and settings gear as ivory outlines in the same row. The verdict headline in Perpetua, ivory,
left-aligned at the content margin, its baseline about 24 px above the horizon axis at 63.3% of the frame. The
Earth's night limb (NASA, ISS) lands exactly on the axis, the dark planet with city lights filling the frame below
it. A mono plate caption bottom left. The lead sentence and best deal begin on the paper just under the frame.

FORM: Title-card dome on the axis, the first device in the system's table; the brief pinned the world, so no seed
roll ran (seed key: none, brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md,
and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
